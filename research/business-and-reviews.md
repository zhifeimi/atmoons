## 2. Existing Website — NO live official website exists

**Verdict: No official website is live.** Evidence:

1. **Google Maps lists no standalone website.** The "Website" field on the listing points to `https://www.bopple.app/arielsdapto` — a third-party (Bopple) online-ordering page, "Built for Ariel's, Dapto © Bopple Technologies", not a real website. Source: https://www.google.com/maps/place/Ariel's+Dapto/
2. **Instagram bio** (https://www.instagram.com/arielsdapto/) links only to `bopple.app/arielsdapto/bio` (a Bopple-hosted link-in-bio page) — no own domain.
3. **Facebook page** (https://www.facebook.com/ArielsDapto/about/) lists `https://bopple.app/arielsdapto` as its "Website" — again Bopple.
4. **Dapto Mall directory** (https://www.daptomall.com.au/stores-services/ariel-s) has a "Visit the website" button pointing to `https://www.ariels.coffee/` — but that domain **does not resolve**: no A/CNAME/SOA/MX DNS records (dig, 2026-09-19), no HTTP response, and the registry RDAP returns 404 "Object not found" (https://rdap.identitydigital.services/rdap/domain/ariels.coffee) — i.e., the domain is **currently unregistered**. The mall link appears to be an ahead-of-time/planned URL.
5. No TripAdvisor, Zomato, Restaurant Guru, Yelp, or news listings found (searched 2026-09-19; TripAdvisor/DoorDash blocked scraping with 403, but Bing/Google surface no such listings).

**Implication:** the business is renting its web presence from Bopple/Facebook/Instagram. A purpose-built website is genuinely warranted. The name "Ariel's Coffee Dapto" plus the mall's dead link suggest `ariels.coffee` is the intended brand/domain (currently available to register).

## 3. Review Analysis

Google review corpus: 4.1★ / 69 reviews — **polarised**: 70% five-star (48) but 20% one-star (14). The praise is about coffee, food quality and friendly staff; the complaints are about service speed/consistency, price transparency, and staff training. Google's own review-topic chips (counts): coffee 19, food 13, environment 4, training 2, refund process 2, morning 2, joke 2, tomato 2. Source: https://www.google.com/maps/place/Ariel's+Dapto/

Note on access: Google now gates full review browsing behind sign-in ("You're seeing a limited view"); 3 full reviews and the 3 headline quotes are visible logged-out. Quotes below were verified against reviewer profile pages. Facebook shows "Not yet rated (1 review)" — effectively no usable Facebook review corpus (https://www.facebook.com/ArielsDapto/). DoorDash listing exists but is scraper-blocked (403).

### Recurring themes

- **Coffee quality (positive, dominant):** Campos beans; "best coffee in Dapto" raves; coffee is the #1 review topic (19 mentions).
- **Food quality & value (positive):** "high quality, plentiful", praised pancakes/waffles/breakfast; generous portions.
- **Service warmth & family-friendliness (positive):** staff "super friendly", "so patient with our kids"; owner replies personally and warmly.
- **Service speed & training (negative):** 1-star reviews cite slow service, staff "chitchat", lack of training; a "refund process" topic exists (2 mentions).
- **Price transparency (negative, minor):** at least one 1-star review driven by a $7.90 small sausage roll with no visible price at the counter.

## 5. Story / Background

- **What/where:** A kiosk café (K105) on the ground floor of Dapto Mall, between the Coles and Woolworths anchors, at Princes Hwy & Moombara St, Dapto NSW — serving the Illawarra suburb of Dapto (Wollongong LGA). https://www.daptomall.com.au/stores-services/ariel-s
- **Trading at least ~3 years:** oldest located review is 3 years old (Peter Antony, 1★, revisited 2026-09). Instagram active since June 2024 (oldest surfaced post: chai latte, Jun 8, 2024). https://www.google.com/maps/contrib/103931382985708917955/reviews ; https://www.instagram.com/arielsdapto/
- **Owner/team:** Owner's name is not stated in any located source (the café name presumably references "Ariel", per the client brief, but this is unverified). Public owner replies show a hands-on, warm operator who answers every review and posts holiday hours. The team is described by the owner as "young… hardworking and genuinely passionate". https://www.google.com/maps/place/Ariel's+Dapto/
- **Community role:** family-oriented local ("Hosting families is something we genuinely love"); seasonal community posts (Easter trading hours, Halloween team costumes); runs a loyalty program; participates in Google food ordering and DoorDash; mall positions it as the centre's coffee-and-breakfast stop. Sources: Google Maps owner posts/replies; https://www.facebook.com/ArielsDapto/ ; https://www.bopple.app/arielsdapto/loyalty
- **Reputation shape:** beloved by regulars (48 five-star), with a visible minority (14 one-star) around speed, training and price-at-counter — classic busy-kiosk growing pains.

## 6. Design Implications (what the website should emphasize)

1. **Lead with coffee and the Campos badge.** Coffee is the most-reviewed topic (19 mentions) and the source of the strongest praise ("Best customer service and coffee in dapto!"). Hero section: Campos Coffee + flat-white-from-$5 price point + "order ahead" link.
2. **Showcase the signatures with real photography:** house-made buttermilk pancakes, cinnamon waffles, all-day breakfast, burgers/burritos, the baked-goods cabinet (cheesecakes, Portuguese tarts, sausage rolls). These are what reviewers and the mall directory single out.
3. **Sell the family-friendly, community vibe:** kids menu, "patient with kids" reviews, owner personality (warm, funny replies; holiday posts), loyalty program, catering for events — a "Our Story / Meet the Team" section in the owner's voice fits the brand.
4. **Fix the trust frictions the 1-star reviews surface:** full menu WITH prices, clear opening hours (sources conflict — must be confirmed with the owner), "skip the queue — order via Bopple/DoorDash" messaging, and a visible phone/WhatsApp contact. Price transparency is a named complaint; the site can neutralise it.
5. **Make location effortless:** kiosk-in-mall context (K105, Ground floor, near Coles/Woolworths), Dapto Mall parking, wheelchair accessibility (entrance/seating/car park all accessible), plus code GQ4W+63, embedded map, and pick-up/delivery/dine-in explainer.
